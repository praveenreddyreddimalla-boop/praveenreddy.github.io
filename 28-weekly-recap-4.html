Week 4 Recap: Zero-Days, DR vs BC, Phishing Red Flags, and the Mirai Botnet

Week four covered zero-day exploits and why an unpatched unknown flaw is so dangerous, the real difference between Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity, five red flags that reliably signal phishing, the Mirai botnet's DDoS attack on Dyn, why cloud security is a shared responsibility rather than something you can fully outsource, and how to build a home cybersecurity lab without spending much money.

A month in, the pattern across nearly every topic has been consistent: strong defenses come from layering multiple, ordinary safeguards — MFA, patching, backups, verification habits, redundancy — rather than finding one silver-bullet tool or relying entirely on someone else (a vendor, a cloud provider, an antivirus) to handle it for you.

Question for you: what's one security habit you've changed since following along this month?
