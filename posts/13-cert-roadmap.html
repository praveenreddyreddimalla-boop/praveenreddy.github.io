Certification Roadmap: Security+ vs CEH vs OSCP — Which One First?

Certifications are a common question for anyone entering the field, and the right first choice depends heavily on your starting point and goals — not on which one is objectively "the best."

CompTIA Security+ is widely treated as the standard entry-level certification. It covers broad foundational concepts — networks, threats, cryptography basics, risk management — and is often used as a baseline requirement for entry-level security roles, including many government and defense-adjacent positions.

CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker) sits a step further along, focused specifically on offensive security concepts and the mindset and tools attackers use, generally aimed at people already comfortable with networking and systems fundamentals.

OSCP (Offensive Security Certified Professional) is a hands-on, exam-based certification widely respected in the penetration testing world specifically because it requires actually compromising systems in a timed lab exam rather than answering multiple-choice questions — it's considerably harder and generally pursued after some real hands-on practice, not as a first step.

Key takeaway: if you're new to the field, Security+ is the conventional starting point. If you already have solid IT fundamentals and want to specialize in offensive security, CEH can bridge you toward OSCP later.
