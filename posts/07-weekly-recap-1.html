Week 1 Recap: AI Phishing, Uptime Math, MFA, and Colonial Pipeline

Week one covered a lot of ground: how AI has changed the shape of phishing emails, what "five nines" of uptime actually means in minutes, why MFA takes a minute to set up but blocks the vast majority of account takeovers, why antivirus alone was never meant to be a complete defense, five free tools to start learning with, and how the Colonial Pipeline attack turned an IT incident into a regional fuel crisis.

The common thread across all of it: most security failures trace back to a single weak link — a reused password, a missed update, an unquestioned request — rather than some unstoppable, sophisticated attack. That's actually good news, because it means small, consistent habits close most of the gap.
