Week 2 Recap: RaaS, Load Balancing, Password Managers, and the AWS Outage

Week two moved between the attacker's side and the defender's side: how Ransomware-as-a-Service lowered the skill barrier for launching attacks, how load balancers decide which server handles your request, why a password manager fixes password reuse for good, why the AWS us-east-1 outage became a lesson in single points of failure, why a strong password alone isn't enough, and a roadmap for picking your first security certification.

A recurring theme: whether you're looking at attacks or at outages, the root causes are strikingly similar — over-reliance on a single point (a password, a server, a region) that seemed fine until it wasn't.
