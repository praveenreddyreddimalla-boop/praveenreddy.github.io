Week 3 Recap: Supply Chain Attacks, Failover, Patching, and SolarWinds

Week three centered on trust and how it gets exploited or breaks down: supply chain attacks that ride in through a trusted vendor, the difference between active-active and active-passive failover, why delaying updates is one of the riskiest habits in tech, a full breakdown of the SolarWinds attack, why small businesses remain frequent targets, and a set of free tools for hands-on availability monitoring.

The thread connecting all of it: both security and availability ultimately come down to what you've decided to trust without verifying — a vendor's update, a single server, a password you reused. Closing those gaps is rarely glamorous work, but it's where almost all real risk reduction happens.
